Compatibility and App Intents

Native App Intents connect Tallio with Siri and Shortcuts. iOS 27 adds system actions to search Activity and open ledger entries. Siri AI availability and routing depend on Apple.

Ask Tallio and English expense drafts require Pro, a compatible Apple Intelligence device and an available English on-device model. The core ledger supports iOS 26 and later.

A few good questions.

Who is Tallio for?

Two people sharing household costs: partners, roommates, or anyone who wants one clear shared ledger.

Can I use different currencies?

Choose one currency for your household. Tallio does not convert currencies or mix them within a ledger.

Does it support Polish?

The app supports English and Polish. Ask Tallio and expense drafts currently accept English only.
