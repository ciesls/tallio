01 — The shared view

Know where you stand, at a glance.

Tallio nets every expense and recorded settlement into one live balance. Both people see the same household ledger through iCloud, without a separate Tallio account.

Two people. One household. No scorekeeping.

02 — Add an expense

Record once. Split fairly.

Choose who paid, add a category, and use the default split or adjust each person’s percentage for this expense. A note is there when you need one.

  • Default or per-entry percentage split
  • Categories, subcategories, and notes
  • Settlements recorded as part of the history

03 — Record a settlement

Close the loop, clearly.

When you pay each other back, record the settlement in the ledger so the balance and history stay together. Tallio records the payment; it does not initiate a transfer.

A payment recorded. The history stays whole.

04 — See the pattern · Pro

Understand the month, without a spreadsheet.

Analytics shows who paid what, your monthly pace, the biggest spending driver, and the balance between fixed and variable costs. Clarity when you want it, quiet when you do not.

See what is included →

05 — Budgets · Pro

Give the month a shape.

Set category budgets and see what remains as the month moves. Useful for the categories that tend to drift.

06 — Recurring

Keep the known costs visible.

Review upcoming entries, skip or pause a rule, and let Tallio add due entries when the person who created the household opens Tallio on or after the due date. One active rule is free; unlimited rules with Pro.

Questions, answered

Good to know
before you begin.

Who is Tallio for?

Tallio is designed for two people sharing a household: partners, roommates, or anyone who wants one clear ledger for shared costs.

Does Tallio move money?

No. Tallio records expenses and settlements so you can see the balance. It does not initiate bank transfers or move money between people.

Can I use more than one currency?

Tallio uses one household currency at a time. It does not convert currencies automatically or apply foreign exchange rates to an expense.

What devices and languages are supported?

Tallio is an iPhone app with English and Polish support, targeting iOS 26 and later. It is coming soon to the App Store.
