Guide

Set up in under a minute

No sign-up screen, no email confirmation. Type two names, invite the other phone, start logging. Production access is for one user and one shared household; a second person joins only that same household through its invitation.

  1. 01

    Name the two of you

    Your name appears in every balance and export, so the ledger reads in plain language: “Ada owes”, “Marek → Ada”.

  2. 02

    Share the household

    Send one iCloud invite. From then on both phones see the same entries within seconds — no server in the middle.

  3. 03

    Log the first expense

    Amount on the keypad, tap a category, mark who paid. Add a comment if it needs one — most don’t.

  4. 04

    Settle whenever you like

    Transfer the balance, tap Settle Up, and the counter returns to zero — with the whole month still on record.

FAQ

Straight answers

Anything missing? Support is one email away.

Do I need an account?+

No. Tallio uses your Apple ID and iCloud. There is no Tallio account, no password, and no email list.

Where is my data stored?+

In your own iCloud, via Apple's CloudKit, and shared with the second member of your household through CloudKit Sharing. The developer operates no backend and never receives your entries. If you don't turn on iCloud sync, everything stays on your device.

Can more than two people use it?+

Not today. Tallio is deliberately a two-person ledger — that constraint is what keeps the balance to a single number and the interface this quiet.

Which currencies are supported?+

PLN, EUR, USD and GBP. You choose one for the household; amounts are formatted to that locale throughout, including exports.

Is there an Android or web version?+

No. Tallio is iPhone-first with an iPad build next. Sync depends on CloudKit, so both people need an Apple device.

How do I get my data out?+

Export CSV from Settings. To remove everything, delete individual records in the app, or delete the app and clear its iCloud data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
