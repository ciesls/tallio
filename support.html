Troubleshooting

Try this first

Nearly every report is one of these five. Sync issues in particular are almost always an iCloud setting rather than the app.

Entries aren't appearing on the other phone+

Check that both people are signed into iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on (Settings → [your name] → iCloud). Confirm the household invite was accepted on the second device. Sync runs over the network, so a device in Low Data Mode or Airplane Mode will hold changes until it reconnects.

The balance looks wrong+

The balance nets every expense against who paid, plus any settlements. Open the month in History and check for a duplicate entry or a settlement recorded twice. Deleting the duplicate corrects the balance immediately on both phones.

A recurring rule didn't post+

Rules post when the app next runs after the due date. Open Tallio once and check the Recurring tab — the rule shows its next scheduled date there. A rule that ended or was paused stops posting until you reactivate it.

I paid for Pro but features are locked+

Use Restore Purchases in Settings, signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase. Pro is per Apple ID, not per household — the second person unlocks it separately. If restore fails, email the receipt and it gets sorted manually.

How do I get my data out — or delete it?+

Export CSV from Settings for a full copy of your entries. To delete, remove records in the app, or delete the app and clear its iCloud data under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Full detail in the privacy policy.
