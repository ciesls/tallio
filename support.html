short version.

Most questions come down to sharing, the way a balance is calculated, or how a record appears in the ledger.

How does sharing work?

Tallio uses Apple’s iCloud sharing flow. Both people need to be signed in to iCloud, with iCloud Drive available, and the invited person needs to accept the household invite. Changes sync when the devices are online.

An entry is not appearing on the other phone

Check that both devices are online, signed in to the intended Apple Account, and that the household invitation was accepted. If the device is offline or in Airplane Mode, its changes wait until it reconnects.

Why does the balance look different than expected?

The balance includes who paid each expense, each person’s share, and recorded settlements. Review the relevant entries and check whether an expense or settlement was entered twice.

When does a recurring entry appear?

Due entries are added when the person who created the household opens Tallio on or after the due date, then sync to the other phone. Open the Recurring tab to see the next scheduled date and the rule’s current state.

Can I export my data?

CSV export is a Pro feature. Tallio Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. In Settings, choose Export CSV to export every expense and settlement, across all dates.

How do I use Tallio with Shortcuts and Siri?

Open Settings in Tallio and find the Shortcuts recipes. The grocery and partner-payment guides help you configure the existing Add Expense and Add Settlement actions. You can also check your balance or open a section with Siri. The basic Add Expense action uses an equal 50/50 split; review the selected action’s options when building your shortcut. Household setup and device authentication may be required.

What do Ask Tallio and English expense drafts need?

Both require Tallio Pro and an available English on-device Apple Intelligence model on a compatible iPhone. For spending, invoke “Ask Tallio” and answer its question prompt with one household category and a date range. If interpretation is unavailable, use Analyze Household Spending with explicit category and dates.

For expense drafts, add Draft Expenses from English Text to a shortcut. Tallio opens a review for up to five expenses; check the details and confirm before anything is saved. Unsupported items are left for manual entry. These two features currently accept English only, even when the app uses Polish.

Does Tallio support Siri AI?

Tallio provides App Intents for Siri and Shortcuts, plus iOS 27 system actions to search Activity and open an entry. Siri AI availability and request routing depend on Apple, your device, language and region. See Apple’s Siri AI requirements. Core Tallio actions do not require its English AI features.

How is my data handled?

Tallio uses iCloud and Apple’s sharing infrastructure for the shared ledger. Read the privacy policy for the full explanation of storage, sharing, and deletion.
